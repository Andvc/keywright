# Keywright: glossary and style for translators

The fixed words of the world, and how each language writes the game. `docs/world.md` has the story behind every
name; `locale/README.md` how a translation is made and tested. When a term here and a text disagree, the term wins:
a player meets "the Gilded Guild" in a hundred places and should meet the same name in each.

## Style

**All languages.**
- `{0}`, `{1}`... are filled in by the game: keep every one, each exactly once, in the order your grammar wants. Read
  the source line (`#: scripts/...`) when it isn't clear what goes in one.
- `{price}`, `{fee}`, `{heft}`, `{colour}`, `{clue}`, `{pounds}`, `{articles}`, `{kind}`, `{dream}`, `{act}` are filled
  in later: copy them exactly, untranslated.
- Keep line breaks (`\n`) where the English has them; add none.
- Pieces that the code joins (" and ", ", ") must still read well joined; check the source.
- Labels, buttons and headings are short in English because the room is short: keep them as short as the language
  allows. A heading in capitals stays in capitals (French keeps its accents on capitals: É, À).
- The tone is warm storybook fantasy: plain words, a little humour, nothing archaic for its own sake. People speak
  like people from where they're from (a goblin, a countess, an old uncle); the interface speaks plainly.
- English-only wordplay (riddles, rhymes, names that are puns) is rewritten so it works in your language, keeping
  what the riddle points at: the answer (a kind of thing) must stay the same.

**Français.** Vouvoiement par défaut ; tutoiement pour Bram (votre oncle), Pip (un enfant), Nim, Nettle, Flick et
Tick. Boutons à l'infinitif (« Acheter », « Passer »). Majuscule au premier mot des titres seulement. Espace insécable
(U+00A0) avant ; : ! ? et à l'intérieur des « guillemets ». Apostrophe droite ('). Points de suspension en trois
points (...). Nettle parle d'elle-même à la troisième personne (« Nettle revient toujours. »).

**日本語.** UIは簡潔に（ボタンは「買う」「見送る」）。説明文は です・ます調、ブラムはくだけた年配の口調（〜だ、〜ぞ）、
ニムは軽妙、貴族や役人は丁寧、ネトルは一人称が「ネトル」。句読点は全角（、。！？「」）、数字は半角。単語の間に
空白を入れない。

**简体中文.** 界面用语简洁（按钮“买下”“不买”）。对话用“你”；玩家对贵族、官员说话用“您”。布拉姆口吻亲切随意，
尼姆油滑，贵族与官员文雅，荨麻用自己的名字称呼自己。使用全角标点（，。！？：；“”），数字用半角。

**繁體中文** is made from the Simplified translation with OpenCC's Taiwan phrases and checked by hand: quotes become
「」, and Taiwan's usual words (e.g. 資訊, 軟體) replace the mainland's.
